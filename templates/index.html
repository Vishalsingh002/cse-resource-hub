Terms & Working Conditions

Academic Fair Use, Working Conditions & Community Guidelines

1. Academic Purpose & Free Non-Commercial Access

Quantum Academic Vault is a student-maintained peer initiative created exclusively for educational study and exam preparation. This repository is strictly non-commercial, free of charge, and has zero advertising, monetization, or paywalls.

2. Academic Fair Use & Intellectual Property

Past question papers and course curriculum remain the intellectual property of Quantum University and respective department faculties. Student-authored lecture notes and revision summaries remain the property of their respective student authors, shared under Fair Dealing for educational study purposes.

3. Contributor Working Conditions

By submitting materials to the Vault, contributors warrant that uploads are authentic question papers, syllabus guides, or verified notes, containing no malicious code, malware, or ongoing confidential exam question leaks.

4. Takedown Policy

Faculty members or authors wishing to have specific materials removed may use the Takedown Request option in the footer for prompt review and resolution within 24 hours.

Privacy Policy

Zero Tracking • Student Privacy First • No Invasive Analytics

1. Zero Tracking

We do not track your browsing habits, sell user data, or inject third-party ad network pixels. You can browse, preview, and download all papers without creating an account or providing an email address.

2. Contributor Attribution

When you contribute notes or question papers, only your preferred display name (e.g. “Vishal Singh”) is publicly shown to credit your contribution. Optional contact details or roll numbers are strictly accessible to moderation admins only.
